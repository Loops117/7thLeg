How buying works
Carts by Vendor
Items that are selected for purchase are placed in the users cart and organized by vendor. Once the user is ready, the cart can be committed to the approval process by the user.

Approval Process
Once a cart is committed to a vendor, an agreement is made by the user to pay for the entire cart once approved. If the cart is refused, the user may be notified of any changes that may need to happen to complete the order.

Order Completion
After the cart has been approved, users have 48 hours to complete payment before the cart is subject to the approval process again.
